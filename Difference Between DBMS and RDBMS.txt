Difference Between DBMS and RDBMS

DBMS (Database Management System)
1.Stores and manages data.
2.Can use different data models.
3.Relationships between tables are not necessary.
4.Keys are not required.

Examples: MongoDB, IBM IMS.



RDBMS (Relational Database Management System)

1.Stores data in tables (rows and columns).
2.Supports relationships between tables.
3.Uses Primary Keys and Foreign Keys.
4.Provides better data integrity.

Examples: MySQL, Oracle, SQL Server, PostgreSQL.
