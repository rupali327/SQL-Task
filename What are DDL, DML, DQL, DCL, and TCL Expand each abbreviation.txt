What are DDL, DML, DQL, DCL, and TCL? Expand each abbreviation.

DDL (Data Definition Language)

Purpose: Defines and modifies database structure.
Examples: CREATE, ALTER, DROP, TRUNCATE

DML (Data Manipulation Language)

Purpose: Adds, updates, and deletes data.
Examples: INSERT, UPDATE, DELETE

DQL (Data Query Language)

Purpose: Retrieves data from a database.
Example: SELECT

DCL (Data Control Language)

Purpose: Controls database access and permissions.
Examples: GRANT, REVOKE

TCL (Transaction Control Language)

Purpose: Manages database transactions.
Examples: COMMIT, ROLLBACK, SAVEPOINT
